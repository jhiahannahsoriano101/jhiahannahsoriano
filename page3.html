Advantages and Disadvantages of AI
Benefits and risks of AI in work and daily life

Advantages

  • AI can automate repetitive and time-consuming tasks.
  • It helps people work faster by summarizing information and generating drafts.
  • It supports better decision-making through data analysis and pattern recognition.
  • AI tools can provide 24/7 support and improve productivity across teams.

Disadvantages

  • AI can produce biased or inaccurate results if data or models are poor.
  • It raises privacy and security concerns when personal or sensitive data is used.
  • Overreliance on AI may reduce human judgment and critical thinking.
  • Some jobs may change or disappear as automation becomes more common.
