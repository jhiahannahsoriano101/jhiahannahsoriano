Popular AI office tools
Used across departments
  • Writing ChatGPT

    Helps generate emails, reports, meeting notes, and summaries so teams can work faster and focus on decision-making.

  • Writing Grammarly

    Improves clarity, grammar, and tone in professional communication before messages are sent to colleagues or clients.

  • Research Perplexity

    Finds answers quickly, compares sources, and gives concise summaries to support planning and research tasks.

  • Scheduling Calendly

    Reduces scheduling back-and-forth by allowing teams and clients to book meetings based on availability in real time.

  • Data Microsoft Excel Copilot

    Helps analyze spreadsheets, generate formulas, and spot trends more efficiently for office reporting and decision support.

  • Support Zendesk

    Improves customer service by automating support tasks, organizing tickets, and speeding up response times.

  • Ethics Responsible AI

    Encourages fairness, transparency, privacy, and human oversight so AI use remains safe and reliable in the workplace.
